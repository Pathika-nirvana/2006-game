# Project art and delivery rules

- Every art task serves the nostalgia of a Korean neighborhood in 2006. This is the highest visual priority. Follow the user's remembered everyday details over generic contemporary food/lifestyle imagery.
- Slush is served in a tall narrow opaque white disposable paper cup without a plastic lid. Cup tteokbokki uses an ordinary small white single-use paper cup. No contemporary cafe packaging or transparent slush cups.
- Preserve the same boy/girl identity, body scale, palette and foot anchor. Exactly two playable people, three outfits each. Preserve existing world and 12-player rooms.
- Eating has four dedicated food families (tteokbokki cup, battered Korean hotdog, rice-cake skewer, slush) and one generic hand-to-mouth family for everything else. Red/blue slush are color variants.
- Draw food, hands, mouth opening, bite and chewing together in coherent sprites. Never erase a rectangular region from the torso to make room for an overlaid food icon.
- Holding food must cover standing, walking, jumping and sitting. Do not deform individual leg joints to invent a gait; retain authored walking frames.
- Do not automatically generate videos, GIFs or long frame-by-frame demonstrations. Use still PNG review sheets and focused tests unless the user explicitly asks for motion media.
- Do not add experimental buttons, rollback/comparison UI, or a physics lab. Do not deploy over the live GitHub/Render service without user approval.
